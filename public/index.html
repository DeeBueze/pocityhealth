Diagnostics that reach the patient, not the other way round.

PO-City builds low-cost point-of-care tests in Lagos. Our goal is simple: fast, affordable results at the bedside, in the clinic and in the community, using parts and materials we can source locally.

Why point of care

Many tests still depend on a trip to a central lab, trained staff and expensive reagents. We build devices that move that work to where care actually happens.

  • Affordable

    Cheap sensing materials and common electronics keep the cost of each test low.

  • Local

    Components are priced and sourced from Lagos suppliers, so the product can be rebuilt and repaired here.

  • Simple to use

    Add a sample, wait, read the screen. No lab training is needed to understand the result.
